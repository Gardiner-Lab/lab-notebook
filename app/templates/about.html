Trellis Tools Lab Notebook

Lab Notebook {{ version }}

Trellis Tools' digital lab notebook: a notebook of days and pages for everyone, a library of protocols, shared between labs as they choose, with active tables, MoClo reactions, and links to plasmids that open in the Plasmid Editor.

Built on Trilium's design

Its structure follows Trilium Notes (TriliumNext; originally by zadam), a hierarchical note-taking application: content is kept in notes, and where a note sits in the tree is kept apart from it, so one note can appear in several places; notes carry labels and relations; every change is kept as a revision; and days are filed under their year and month as in Trilium's journal. The Lab Notebook is a separate program written for the lab suite, with its own code; it shares Trilium's ideas, not its source.
